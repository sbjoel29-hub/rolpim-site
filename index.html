A Word From The Author

My Personal Testimony

The year 2024 has been the hardest year of my life, as of this writing. This book was born out of my personal battle against the spirit of death that came against my health.

Every word written in these pages came as the Spirit of life in Jesus Christ illuminated to me the path of life revealed in the Scriptures, so that I could freely walk out of the tyrannous reign of death that has already been abolished…

That same fight against the spirit of death entered our ministry, creating misunderstandings and quarrels that even led to separation from some disciples who had become family to us… Indeed, it is through many tribulations that we enter into the experience and possession of the inheritance of the Kingdom life in Christ Jesus.

To the praise of God, my bodily health has begun springing up speedily, as willows by the watercourses, through the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus Christ from the dead. Hallelujah!

“You did your best to kill me, O my enemy, but the Lord helped me.”Psalm 118:13 (NLT)

Meet The Pastors

Pastor Collins O. Ben‑Zion & Pastor Mrs Mercy Ben‑Zion

Witness Onyebuchi Collins Ben‑Zion is a man called and sent by God to pursue and herald the reign of life and immortality on earth, to a world ravished by death. Through the priestly ministry of the Word of life and prayer, he is commissioned by Christ to raise a company of Priestly Bride that will live the Christ life in this present world and reign with Him in the millennium.

He has been faithful to this vision for a decade alongside his wife, Mercy Ben‑Zion. He is the set‑man of Reign of Life Priesthood International Ministry, in Abuja, Nigeria. They are blessed with four children.
